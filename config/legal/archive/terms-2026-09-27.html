3. The free plan

Every account starts on the free plan. It is free permanently, and it has these limits:

Documents completed per month 5
Documents sent per month 15
Documents waiting for signatures at any one time 10
Users 1
Document storage Included, subject to fair use

The monthly limits run on the UTC calendar month and reset by themselves at midnight UTC on the first of each month. A few things are worth spelling out:

  • What counts as a completed document. A document counts the first time any signer completes it. A document with several signers counts once, not once per person. Corrections, resends and re-completions of the same document never add another completion. A document that is declined, or that expires with nobody having signed it, counts nothing.
  • What counts as a document sent. Every document created for signature counts as a send, however it was created — the recipients form, a share link, an API call, “sign it yourself” or a correction. Deleting or archiving a document afterwards does not give the send back.
  • Storage, and fair use. We store the documents you keep with us: your uploads and templates, the signed documents and audit trails we produce for you, the files and signatures your signers add, and your logo — including anything in your archive, until you delete it permanently. Storage is included in every plan, subject to fair use. If your account gets close to the fair-use limit, we email your administrators first; if it reaches the limit, new uploads by people in your account pause until space is freed. Storage never stops a document being sent, and it never stops a signer from signing.
  • Documents already sent always complete. Reaching a limit never strands a document that is already out for signature. The people holding it can still finish it, and that completion is still recorded.
  • Share links pause at the limit. When the free plan's monthly limit is reached, share links stop accepting new documents until the month resets. Nothing is deleted and nothing is lost.
  • The free plan carries our footer. Emails we send on your behalf and the pages your signers sign on show an EsignCenter footer.
  • Some features are on the paid plan only. The API, webhooks, embedded signing, signing sessions, automatic reminders, conditional logic, custom email templates, sending from your own email server, BCC, delivery tracking and removing our branding all need a paid plan. The current list is on our pricing page.

Correcting a document somebody has already signed

Sometimes a document is signed and only then does somebody spot a mistake. You fix it and send the same document out again, and we treat that as the same document rather than a new one.

Correcting a signed document and sending it again never uses a second completion, however many rounds it takes; it still counts as a send.

Correcting a signed document is allowed even when you have reached the monthly completion limit; it still counts as a send and as an open document.

If the person you invite already has their own EsignCenter account, accepting your invitation moves them into your team: their templates, folders and documents come with them, and their old account is archived. A failed payment does not take the service away on the first day.

  • We email the account's administrators four times in that period: on the day the payment fails, then three days, a week and thirteen days later. Every one of those emails says when the account will be suspended, or that it already has been, and links straight to the billing page.
  • On day 14 the account is suspended.

Suspended means read-only, and nothing more than that:

  • Everybody can still sign in. Nothing is deleted.
  • Every document, template and export stays readable, downloadable and exportable.
  • A signer who already holds one of your documents can still finish signing it, and that completion is still recorded.
  • Nothing new can be created or changed — documents, templates, people, settings — and the API and webhooks stop.
  • Paying lifts the suspension automatically, within seconds. There is no support ticket and no manual step.

If our card processor reports the subscription as unpaid or paused rather than merely late, there is no grace left to give and the account is suspended straight away, in exactly the read-only shape above. Nothing is deleted, everything stays readable and exportable, and settling the payment lifts the suspension automatically. Later on, if the card processor gives up on the subscription altogether and cancels it, there is nothing left to collect: the account returns to the free plan and can write again under the free plan's limits.

A permanent deletion is not quite total, and we would rather you knew exactly what is left before you press the button. This is the whole list:

  • Our own log of support access to the account — when we looked, why, and what we did. It is the record that makes that access answerable, so deleting it on request would defeat its purpose.

Nothing else survives. Your documents, templates, folders, audit trails, uploaded and signed files, signatures, settings, API keys, invitations and logins are all destroyed, and the email addresses on those logins are released for anyone to use again.
